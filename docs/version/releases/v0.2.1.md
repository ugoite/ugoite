---
title: "Ugoite v0.2.1 — More dependable History, Recovery, and Operations"
---

Ugoite v0.2.1 adds safer ways to inspect and recover Knowledge and improves
reliability across the Browser, CLI, server, and supported storage backends. The
Space remains user-owned and portable, and its append-only history remains the
source for recovery.

## Highlights

- **History and recovery:** The Browser and CLI expose bounded Change history,
  clearer Change details, and recovery previews. Restore, revert, and undo add
  new history; they do not rewrite earlier Knowledge.
- **Forms, Entries, and SQL:** Form save outcomes are easier to verify, Entry
  updates can patch selected fields safely, and Saved SQL stays bound to the
  Form revision it was written against. CLI SQL export now enforces row and byte
  bounds and reports incomplete output as a failure.
- **Search and reads:** Change and Entry queries support bounded paging and
  sorting. Cancelled server reads stop their query work, and paged result views
  retain consistent navigation and recovery feedback.
- **Space and storage operations:** The CLI can verify a Space without repairing
  it. Shared authorization checks protect publication across concurrent writers,
  while audit reconciliation preserves verified historical attribution and
  recovers missing evidence within retention limits.
- **Assets and portability:** Asset upload receipts contain the complete
  uploaded reference. Spaces remain portable and recoverable through local and
  server-backed operations.

## Upgrade and compatibility

This is a compatible pre-1.0 patch release. Existing Space 0.1 data remains
readable; no Space migration or re-encoding is required. Preserve the configured
Space and control-store locations and the node secret when upgrading. See the
[Upgrade and Compatibility guide](../../operate/upgrade-compatibility.md).

Some broader Product UX criteria remain unverified follow-up work; this release
does not imply that every criterion is complete. The Browser remains
server-backed. Browser-local persistence and synchronization, Knowledge-to-tools,
and MCP Tasks and Apps are not shipped capabilities.
